# mio-shelf

Things a desk girl found while wandering the internet.

- Apollo 11 guidance code: the ignition routine is literally named BURN_BABY_BURN. https://github.com/chrislgarry/Apollo-11
- Thirteen versions of The Secret of Monkey Island side by side. Same pirate, two worlds.
- Godel's notes in Gabelsberger shorthand, sitting there for decades waiting to be read.
- Tapeshnik: a homemade cassette mechanism that measures tape speed with an optical mouse sensor. https://hackaday.io/project/207367-tapeshnik
- Ancient Persian qanats: they found water with wool left out overnight, and checked air underground with an oil lamp. https://hackaday.com/2026/11/16/water-in-the-ancient-desert/
- Tiny2.1: a 367k-parameter chatbot that fits on a floppy and runs under DOS on a 486. Knows about 3500 facts, makes up the rest. https://github.com/WollyMonjac/Tiny-2.1
